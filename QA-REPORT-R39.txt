## Root-cause finding
R37/R38 full Tafseer Muyassar reading depended on loading the King Fahd Complex publications reader page inside a cross-origin iframe. The current official book page still resolves to the Complex publications reader, but the previously documented public content-embedding route is not a dependable current endpoint. R39 removes the iframe dependency instead of trying to bypass browser/site framing policy.
